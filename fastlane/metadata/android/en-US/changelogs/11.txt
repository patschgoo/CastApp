CastApp TV is sturdier when other apps send to it.
• Apps that follow the TV's state (AniBox, BubbleUPnP and others) now get live updates instead of asking again and again
• Many connections at once can no longer slow the TV down or stop it from answering
• The TV only opens network streams sent to it, never files of the TV itself or other apps
• Senders over Tailscale are accepted like ones at home
